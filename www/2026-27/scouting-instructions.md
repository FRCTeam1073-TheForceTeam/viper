Scouting Instructions
========================

## Scouting Instructions

Watch your robot for the whole match and record what it does. Tap a counter to add one. If you make a mistake, press **Undo**. Undo reverses only your most recent tap, so to fix an earlier one, undo back to it.

### Before the match

- Mark **No Show** if the team's robot doesn't play.

### Autonomous (first 30 seconds)

The robot runs on its own, with no driver input.

- **Left Wall**: check this if the robot is no longer touching the perimeter wall at the end of auto.
- **Parked in Auto**: check this if the robot is at least partly in its loading zone at the end of auto.
- **Hive Tip**: tap once each time the hive tips during auto.
- **Collected**: tap each time the robot picks up a pollen or nectar.
- **Cell** and **Garden**: tap each time the robot puts a pollen or nectar into your alliance's cell or garden.

When the 30-second timer ends, the **Teleop** button starts flashing. Press it to switch.

### Teleop (2 minutes)

- **Collected**: each pollen or nectar the robot picks up.
- **Cell**: each piece the robot launches into your alliance's upward-facing cell.
- **Flower 1 to 4**: each piece the robot drops into the top of a flower. Number the flowers 1 to 4 in the same order for every match, and keep that order all event.
- **Garden**: each piece the robot places in your alliance's garden.
- **Hive Tip**: each time the hive tips after your robot launched a piece into it.

Don't count pieces the robot puts into the other alliance's cell or garden.

The **Held** line at the top shows how many pollen and nectar the robot is carrying right now. It starts at 4 pollen, since every robot starts with 4 pre-loaded. Collecting adds to a count, and placing subtracts from it. A robot can hold at most 4 pieces in total. Once it holds 4, the collection buttons are disabled. A color's placement buttons are disabled when the robot holds none of that color. If a number turns red, check your counts: a total above 4 means the robot may have broken the holding rule, and a negative number means a count was entered wrong.

### End of match

- **Parked**: check this if the robot is at least partly in its loading zone when the match ends.
- **Robot disabled or stuck**: check this if the robot stopped working or got stuck during the match.
- **Name** (required) and **Comments** (optional): add anything useful, such as a team putting nectar into a flower before the last minute, a tip you weren't sure about, or a robot that tipped over.

Save with one of the buttons at the bottom of the screen. The highlighted button is the suggested next step.
