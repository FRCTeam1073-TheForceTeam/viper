Stats Instructions
========================

### Reading a Boxplot

![](/boxplot-help.png)

<div class=eli5Detailed>

 - Maximum: The biggest value
 - 75th Quartile: A quarter of the values are bigger and three-quarters are smaller
 - Median: Half the values are bigger, and half are smaller
 - Mean: The average value
 - 25th Quartile: Three-quarters of the values are bigger and a quarter are smaller
 - Minimum: The smallest value

</div>

<div class=eli5Simple>

The thin line covers every match this team has played, from their worst at the bottom to their best at the top. The fat box in the middle is where half of their matches landed. A short box means they do about the same thing every time; a tall box means you cannot count on which team will show up.

 - **Maximum** — The best they have ever done. Exciting, but do not plan on it.
 - **75th Quartile** — A good match for them. They beat this about one match in four.
 - **Median** — Their normal match. Half are better, half are worse. This is the number to plan around.
 - **Mean** — Every match added up and shared out evenly. If it sits well above the median, a few big matches are doing the lifting and most of their matches fall short of it — they are less consistent than the average makes them look.
 - **25th Quartile** — A rough match for them. They score below this about one match in four.
 - **Minimum** — The worst they have ever done. Worth asking whether that was a breakdown or just a bad day.

Picking between two teams with the same median? Take the one with the shorter box. They are the safer partner.

</div>
